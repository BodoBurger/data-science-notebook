## What is MyST?

MyST stands for "Markedly Structured Text". It is a slight variation on a flavor of markdown called CommonMark markdown,
with small syntax extensions to allow you to write **roles** and **directives** in the Sphinx ecosystem.

You can learn more at https://mystmd.org/guide.
